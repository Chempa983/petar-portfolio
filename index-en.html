01 / THE PERSON BEHIND THE ROLE

I've worked on the shop floor. Now I want to improve the bigger picture.

I know what a normal shift looks like when deliveries must move, a machine stops, or information fails to reach the right person.

I've worked as a forklift driver, machine operator and process operator. Those roles taught me that quality, safety and teamwork require constant attention.

My earlier background also includes hospitality, restaurants and event operations. That taught me how to organise people, communicate clearly and stay calm under pressure.

I trained as a project manager because I wanted to take the next step: from solving the problems of today to planning and driving long-term improvement.
