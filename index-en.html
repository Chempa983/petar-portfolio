03 / DIGITAL TOOLS IN REAL OPERATIONS

Empiri X3. A tool I use every day.

HANDS-ON SYSTEM EXPERIENCE

In my current role, I work with Empiri X3, primarily the Truck module. I have approximately one year of hands-on experience using it to navigate material movements.

I locate material, check storage positions, take measurements, record relevant information and document where materials are placed. It helps me work systematically and connect system information to physical material handling.

I also have access to the history of material movements in the system. This gives me a starting point for investigating repeated relocations using earlier records. I have not yet conducted a systematic review of that history or measured any improvement results.
